Wisconsin Software Development Club

Our purpose

Practical skills.
Shared experience.

Our goal is simple: help our members become better software developers.

We do that by creating space to work on projects together, learn practical skills in workshops, and hear from developers working in the industry.

Along the way, we practice the parts of development that extend beyond code: collaboration, responsibility, problem solving, and sharing what we’ve learned.
